## 📌 Problem Statement

Traditional Indian wear (ethnic outfits, jewellery, accessories) is worn briefly but priced for permanent ownership — creating three recurring pain points:

- **High one-time cost** for outfits used only once or twice (weddings, festivals, cultural events)
- **Fragmented, offline discovery** — local rental shops exist but have no digital visibility, forcing buyers toward expensive, brand-heavy alternatives
- **Low trust in local/unbranded vendors** — no standard way to verify hygiene, condition, or reliability before renting from an unfamiliar shop

This traps small local vendors in obscurity while pushing customers toward costly, impersonal options — a solvable inefficiency, not a lack of supply.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**O2O Tradition** is a hyperlocal, trust-first marketplace connecting customers to nearby verified vendors for traditional wear — enabling:

- **Rent** — short-term wear for events, without the cost of ownership
- **Buy** — for those who want to own
- **Sell** — vendors and individuals monetize unused inventory
- **Swap** — peer-to-peer exchange for sustainable reuse

Core differentiators:
- **Hyperlocal discovery** — nearby-first, so local/"unsung" vendors get visibility they currently lack
- **Trust Protocol** — verified vendor onboarding and a hygiene/condition assurance layer, so customers can trust an unfamiliar local shop
- **Digital record-keeping** — replaces the manual, paper-based tracking most small rental vendors still rely on
